Keep it safe

Your jars live in this browser only. A backup file is a spare copy you keep yourself.

Save a copy

Downloads one small file holding every cookie in the jar that is open. Keep it wherever you keep important files. Your jar here stays as it is.

Put saved cookies back in

Use this after losing your jar, on a new device, or when someone sends you their cookies. The cookies in the file are added. Nothing already here is removed or replaced, and a cookie you already have is not added twice.

Where should the cookies go?

A backup of all your jars puts each one back under its own name.

Cookie Jar is free and open source. It has no server and no tracking. Your cookies are stored by your browser on this device and nowhere else. If you clear your browser data, your jars are emptied, so keep a backup.

Put it on your phone. On an iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen". On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app". The jar then opens from its own icon and works without a connection. A jar on your phone is separate from a jar on your computer; use a backup file to move cookies between them.
