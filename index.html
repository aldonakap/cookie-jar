Downloads one small file holding every cookie in the jar. Keep it wherever you keep important files. Your jar here stays as it is.

Put saved cookies back in

Use this after losing your jar, on a new device, or when someone sends you their cookies. The cookies in the file are added to this jar. Nothing already here is removed or replaced, and a cookie you already have is not added twice.

Cookie Jar is free and open source. It has no server and no tracking. Your cookies are stored by your browser on this device and nowhere else. If you clear your browser data, the jar is emptied, so keep a backup.
